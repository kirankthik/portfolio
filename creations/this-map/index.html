This Map

Drawing no. 001 · a résumé you can wander around in

  1. The brief

    A map of a career. Isometric, like a diagram. Free to roam. It should feel freeing to whoever sees it, and it should have room for everything that gets made next.

  2. The method

    A technical writer described the feeling. An AI asked questions, wrote the code, looked at its own screenshots and adjusted. No 3D software, no assets, no engineer.

  3. The world

    Every island, tree, cow and lighthouse is built from a handful of simple shapes at the moment the page loads. The whole map is described in one small text file.

  4. The sky

    One shader. Its hour follows the trail, so the story is told by the light: night at the start, first light at the second degree, sunrise where the writing begins, morning at the frontier.

  5. The lens

    A soft glow on every lamp and a tilt-shift blur at the edges make it read as a miniature. On slower devices both switch themselves off.

  6. The two voices

    The same career is written twice: once plainly, as a résumé, and once honestly, as islands. The line drawing on the résumé is generated from the same map.

  7. The rule

    Each creation gets its own island and its own page, and each is allowed to look nothing like the others. This page is a blueprint because this one is about building.

Parts list: Three.js · one orthographic camera · hexagons · two shaders · two typefaces · conversation.
